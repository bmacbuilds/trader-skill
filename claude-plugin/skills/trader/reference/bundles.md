# Export, import and backup: in a later release

Exporting, backing up, sharing or importing research as a portable bundle is **not in this release**. It is
coming in a later release. In this release `traderos export` and `traderos import` (its preview and its apply)
answer `CAPABILITY_DISABLED` (exit 3): not in this release.

## When the person asks

- Say it plainly: export, import and backup are coming in a later release, and are not available now.
- Offer nothing in their place. Do not run those commands to see what happens, and never stand in for them: no
  copy of the store's files, no archive of the TraderOS folder, and no bundle or Passport written by you.
- Their research stays in their local store, where every record can be reopened by its id
  ([strategy-lab.md](strategy-lab.md)).

## What it never does

It never exports, shares or imports anything in this release.
